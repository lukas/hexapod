# Femur yoke: 7 mm arms

Based on horn-compression-limiters v25 following reported side-arm cracking.
Both femur yoke arms increase from 4 to 7 mm, growing outward by 3 mm.
The inner servo gap, horn pads, screw and spacer seats stay unchanged.
Recesses preserve the existing washers and 10 mm spacer stack. The
connecting wall remains at its already-reinforced thickness; the arm
additions run through the root to its outer face at X52.

Retains both prior 2 mm extensions, the tibia's AprilTag clearances and
closed tube socket. Knee holder, clamp cap and inserts remain unchanged.
Only the femur needs reprinting. The 3MF plate contains geometry and
orientation, not printer/material settings.

Checks cover single closed geometry, original material retention,
unchanged inner gap and knee holder, clear spacer and screw passages,
watertight exported STL and 3MF round trip. Physical strength and full
robot articulation are not tested.
