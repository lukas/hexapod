Sensor foot v9 — one prototype, two material plates

PETG plate: rounded foot + rod cylinder.
TPU plate: return collar + contact pad.

Open each 3MF as geometry in your slicer, retain the supplied orientation, and choose your own printer and material profile. These are unsliced geometry plates, not printer-specific projects or G-code. All parts are in millimeters, on Z=0, with 10 mm between their bounding boxes. One of each part is included.

Cylinder is pins-up; rounded foot is flat sensor-seat down. TPU collar is lower-cuff down; contact pad is flat. Inspect the cylinder bore bridge and TPU collar overhangs in sliced preview. Review support placement so the guide bores remain clear. Bench-test this single set before printing six.
