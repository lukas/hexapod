# Tibia tube socket back wall

Based on spacer build v24. Close the inner end of the tibia's 8.1 mm
socket bore with a 2 mm wall (joint-local X44–46). The 8 mm carbon tube
enters from X64 and seats at X46, leaving 18 mm engagement. The stop
prevents inward push-through; it does not prevent pulling the tube out.

Both 2 mm yoke extensions, 7 mm tibia arms, AprilTag cup clearances and
all screw/spacer interfaces remain unchanged. Only the tibia yoke needs
reprinting. The print plate contains geometry, not printer settings.

Validation checks original material preservation, unchanged exterior,
free tube entry, a solid stop across the tube end, a closed exported STL
and the 3MF round trip. Physical fit and wall strength remain untested.
