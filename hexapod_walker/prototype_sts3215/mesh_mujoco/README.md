# mesh_mujoco — the as-built robot in MuJoCo

Every printed part plus the bought hardware (servos, disc horns, bearings, CF
tubes, TPU boots, LiPo packs, the electronics stack) at the verified CAD
assembly transforms, used for rendering and collision. This is the default
`env.model_source: mesh` that training and evaluation use (`rl_move/sim/
servo_model.py`); `mujoco_prototype.py` is the capsule/box approximation.

## What is here

- `assets/*.stl` + `hexapod_mesh.xml` — the full mesh model. **Tracked build
  products**: generated by `mesh_mujoco/build_mesh_model.py` in the CAD repo
  https://github.com/lukas/hexapod-cad and copied here (`make mesh-sync` there).
  Do not hand-edit; regenerate from the CAD and commit the result together with
  any dimension change in `hexapod_core/geometry.py`.
- `hexapod_mesh_mjx.xml` — self-contained MJX twin (same kinematics, masses and
  inertia, primitive collision) used where the STL assets would be too slow or
  are absent.
- `view_mesh.py` / `view_mesh.sh` — interactive viewer: `./view_mesh.sh plant`
  or `./view_mesh.sh stance` (needs `mjpython` on macOS).
- `previews/` — renders of the two keyframes.
